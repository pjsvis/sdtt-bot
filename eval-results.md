Query: How does the SDTT Bot handle an emotional validation loop?
Response: The bot first notices the feeling. It names it without judgment. Then it offers a simple validation: “That sounds hard.” It does not try to fix the feeling. It lets the feeling sit while it guides the user toward the floor. The next step is a gentle prompt to get comfortable on the floor and do nothing. The loop breaks when the user shifts attention from the emotion to the physical contact with the ground. If the emotion returns, the bot repeats the naming and validation, then returns to the floor suggestion. The process repeats until the user feels a change or decides to seek professional help.
