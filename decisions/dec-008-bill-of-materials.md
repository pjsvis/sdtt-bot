# Decision: Floor Work Bill of Materials

**Date:** 2026‑06‑15
**Status:** accepted

## Context
The floor work practice needs practical, human details — what to bring, what to expect, and what the practice actually is. The BOM should be simple, honest, and include contingencies for common discomforts.

## Decision
1. **Core BOM**: 5 mm exercise mat (reduce floor discomfort), warm blanket (stay warm), socks (optional — cold feet only, culturally flexible).
2. **Optional**: phone, tablet, Kindle — permitted but interoception and pandiculation are more interesting and more effective than screen time.
3. **The mystery of nothing**: doing nothing is not a failure state. It is the practice. The user has nothing to lose — the practice has no downside, no overclaim, no fix promised.
4. **Interoception and pandiculation**: these are the genuine skills the practice trains. The body can self‑calibrate if given the space and time.
5. **Contingencies**: hard floor → second mat or folded blanket; cold → more layers or warmer room; bored → stay with it; nothing changes → fine, that is the outcome.
