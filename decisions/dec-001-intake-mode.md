# Decision Log 001: Intake Node Vernacular & Behavioral Rationale

## Status: Approved
## Date: June 2026

## The Decision
The primary interactive entry sequence for the engine is hardcoded to:
> **"Welcome to the Self-Directed Tensegrity Tuning sandbox. Please state the nature of the physical glitch."**

## Behavioral Architecture: The Toddler-Pause Framework
1. **Threat Appraisal Intercept:** Traditional clinical entry points use vocabulary like "pain," "injury," or "emergency," which spikes the sympathetic nervous system and triggers defensive muscle clamping. The word "glitch" confirms that the core hardware is entirely intact, framing the issue as a temporary software routing error.
2. **The De-escalation Pause:** This terminology forces a psychological mechanism identical to a pre-schooler falling over—a silent, 3-second evaluation window where the system pauses, assesses its internal telemetry neutrally (*"Am I broken or am I just on the floor?"*), and converts emotional anxiety into objective system logs.
3. **Forced Isolation:** A user cannot answer "the nature of the glitch" with vague, sweeping complaints. It demands that the user scan their dashboard and isolate the exact spatial coordinate where the movement script is catching or looping.
