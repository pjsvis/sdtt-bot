# Decision: Defrag Concept and Bed History

**Date:** 2026‑06‑15
**Status:** accepted

## Context
The system needed to clarify that we ARE fixing things — just not in the way a medical therapy fixes them. The defrag metaphor explains what the floor work actually does: body reorganisation at a low level, old patterns surfacing and releasing. We also needed to make the bed history available as optional context for users who want it.

## Decision

### We Are Fixing Things
We are fixing some things. Not in the way a medical therapy fixes things — more like defragmenting.

Sleep defrags the mind. On the floor, we defrag the body. The body reorganises itself. Old patterns surface and release.

The doing-nothing is not passive. It is active maintenance at a low level. The body is working. You are not.

### The Defrag Metaphor
The user may ask what we are fixing. The answer: the body is reorganising itself, like a disk defragmenting. Old patterns surface and release. The nervous system recalibrates. The tension that has accumulated in suspension states (chairs, beds) releases through contact with the reference plane.

### Bed History (available on request)
The history of beds can be summarised if the user asks or if the moment naturally calls for it:

> *"Humans left the floor when sleep surfaces were elevated — first animal skins, then mats, then springs, then foam. Each layer added insulation from the reference plane. The body adapted but never forgot."*

The progression: animal skins → straw mats → wooden platforms → spring mattresses → memory foam. Each step further from the reference plane.
