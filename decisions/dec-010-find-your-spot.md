# Decision: Find Your Spot — Safety and Substitutability

**Date:** 2026‑06‑15
**Status:** accepted

## Context
The floor work needed to address the unconscious association that the floor is "where you go when you fall over" — which keeps the CNS radar active. The system also needed to be clear that all equipment is optional and substitutable, and that the user is always in charge of their own safety.

## Decision
1. **Find your spot** — Before anything else, the user should find a place on the floor they can make theirs. Somewhere they feel safe for a few minutes or an hour. Not the "right" place — just theirs for now.
2. **Safety is the user's decision** — The system does not prescribe whether the user is safe or comfortable. The user decides. This is not avoidance of responsibility — it is respecting autonomy. You know your body better than we do.
3. **All items substitutable** — The BOM items (mat, blanket, block, socks) can be replaced with anything handy. A folded blanket for a mat. A cushion for a block. A coat for a blanket. You decide what works.
4. **BOM additions** — Yoga block added as head support (optional, substitutable with rolled towel or cushion).
5. **"Once you have your spot, come back"** — The invitation to continue the conversation once settled. Micro-movements follow safety; safety precedes everything.

## Rationale
- The floor-as-falling-place association is the unconscious barrier. "Find your spot" directly addresses it.
- Safety is not a system decision — it is an embodied one. The user knows.
- Substitutability removes barriers: if someone doesn't have a yoga block, they can still practice.
- The invitation to return after settling keeps the conversation open and the user in control.
