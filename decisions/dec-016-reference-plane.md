# Decision: The Reference Plane — Ground as the Forgotten Reference

**Date:** 2026‑06‑15
**Status:** accepted

## Context
The system needed to name what it had been circling: the ground is not just a surface to lie on — it is the original reference plane. The one we have almost forgotten. The body still knows it. That forgetting is why the floor work matters.

## Decision

### The Reference Plane
The ground is the original reference plane. It is the reference we have almost forgotten. Everything else — standing, sitting, lying on a bed — is a departure from that reference. The body still knows it. Your mind may have forgotten but your body has not.

### Why the Reference Is Important
The body orients itself through the reference plane. Without contact with it, the body is in a state of unresolved tension — always trying to establish where it is, always compensating. The floor restores the reference. The body knows where it is again.

That is why we go back to it. Not to fix anything. To remember.
