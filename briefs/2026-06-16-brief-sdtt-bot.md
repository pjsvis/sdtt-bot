## Deployment Vector Assessment

Regarding the final deployment architecture, a clean, open-access web chatbot is vastly superior to an smart speaker or voice skill for two specific reasons:

1. **The Telemetry Display Limit:** Voice interfaces lack spatial clarity. A user cannot easily parse a complex cross-axial dependency map or scan a structural hypothesis via audio while lying on a floor mat.
2. **The Citation Verification Block:** The **narrativised bibliography** is our primary cognitive anchor. Reading out academic citations and text references via an audio loop introduces massive friction and destroys the clean, authority-cached shorthand. A crisp text screen allows the user to see the statement-explanation-reference pipeline instantly, lowering their threat appraisal before they drop to the floor.
